Dance floor Lighting

When it comes to weddings, the dance floor is the heart of the party.

It's where memories are made, and where you and your guests will spend the majority of your reception. At BLE, we understand the importance of creating the perfect dance floor experience for your friends and family. That's why we offer the Intelligent Dance Floor Lighting (or Intel Lights for short) enhancement that will take your reception to the next level.

With a combination of state-of-the-art LED lights, strobe lights, moving head lights and RGB colors, our Intel Lights will create a  nightlife experience that will have your guests dancing all night long. This is not just about adding some lights to your reception, this is about creating the perfect atmosphere for you and your guests to let loose and have a good time.

Don't settle for generic and bland lighting, trust us to elevate your reception and make it the ultimate party experience, add our Intel Lighting enhancement to your wedding package and get ready to dance the night away.
